How it works

  1. Tap your campus or ministry above.Dropbox's upload page opens in a new tab.
  2. Choose your photos and upload.Original camera files (CR3 or other RAW) are best. Phone photos are welcome too.
  3. Type your name and email when asked.That's only so we know who sent them. You don't need a Dropbox account.
  4. Congrats! You have uploaded your photos for Mitchell to edit!

Lots of photos? Keep the tab open until every file says it's done. Big camera files can take a few minutes on slower Wi-Fi.
